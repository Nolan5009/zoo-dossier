Emu

Scientific Name

Dromaius Novaehollandiae

Status

Least Concern

Physical Appearance

Emu's have small useless wings in exchange for strong legs. Their feet have 3 toes and less bones and muscles compaired to other birds. Their feathers grow in pairs of two from follicles. They have a pouch in their throat that is a part of their windpipe. They can grow up to 5.7 feet. The males can weigh from 110 pounds to 121 pounds while the females only weigh up to 11 pounds
